Customize the kit

Edit or add to this Cut30 kit to match your preferences. Every change belongs in both the visual HTML preview and the agent-readable JSON.

  1. Give your agent direction. Share your preferred font, title treatment, caption size, or placement. Use these defaults as a starting point.
  2. Update the JSON first. Your agent reads kit.json for the actual values. New fields or sections also need visible HTML rendering and documentation in kit-schema.md.
  3. Keep the preview in sync. Have your agent validate the kit, refresh the HTML’s embedded JSON, and review both aspect ratios. Then log the change.

Cut30 / Cut30.co attribution stays on this kit page. It does not belong on your videos.
