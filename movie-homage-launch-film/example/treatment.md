# Treatment: "I know how"

**Logline.** In a dark loft, an operator searches varg how to and loads the skill it finds into a porcelain agent.
The agent opens its eyes: "I know how to make a music video." "Show me."

**Format.** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~47 s. Announcement for X, LinkedIn and the varg.ai/how-to page.

**Inspired by.** *The Matrix* (1999), the skill-loading scene and the white "construct". Homage, not a copy: our own
characters, our own palette, no names, no footage.

## Structure (3 acts on 47 s)

| act | time | beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · the search | 0–15.5 s | Rain, the loft, the agent in the chair. The operator types "how to make a music video". The real varg how to answer fills the screen. LOAD. The skill streams in. |
| 2 · the proof | 15.5–32 s | Eyes open: "I know how to make a music video." "Show me." Hard cut to white. The agent raises a hand and the world of a music video assembles around it. We see the music video it made. |
| 3 · the catalog | 32–47 s | "What else?" The catalog flicks past: UGC ads, captions, short films, explainers. The agent looks at us: "Just ask." End card: varg how to · varg.ai/how-to. |

## Characters (original)

- **The Agent.** A porcelain android: matte warm-white ceramic face and hands with fine seam lines, sculpted closed
  lips (it never lip-syncs; its voice is inner), eyes of dark glass that catch an ember glint once loaded. Charcoal
  knit sweater, black trousers, bare porcelain hands. A thin cable with an amber-lit collar plugs in at the base of
  the skull.
- **The Operator.** A woman in her forties, cropped silver hair, thin wire-rim glasses, black roll-neck, a worn dark
  wool coat, a one-ear headset. Calm, dry, a half smile.
- **The Singer** (only inside the music video the agent makes). A woman in her twenties, wet black bob, a glossy red
  vinyl coat, in the rain under red and amber neon.

## Locations

- **A · The loft.** Night, rain on tall steel-framed industrial windows, raw concrete, an oxblood leather armchair in
  the middle under a hanging work lamp, a console of three amber-phosphor CRT monitors on the left with cables over the
  floor. The CRTs are the only colour.
- **B · The construct.** An infinite white void, soft shadowless light, a faint reflection on the floor.
- **C · The street.** The music video's world: a narrow wet street at night, red and amber neon (no text on signs),
  steam, puddle reflections.

## Colour script

Black and ember (A) → pure white (B) → red neon (C) → black and ember (A) → varg black end card with the ember accent.

## Sound

Score: slow, dark analog synth pulse with a heartbeat kick; a bass drop and drums on the cut to white; resolves on the
end card. Rain and CRT hum in the loft. Two voices: the Agent (calm, low, slightly synthetic male), the Operator
(dry, warm female).

## Lines

- Agent: "I know how to make a music video."
- Operator: "Show me."
- Operator: "What else?"
- Agent: "Just ask."

## End card

**varg how to** · Teach your agent to make any video. · Skills for Claude Code, Codex and Grok · **varg.ai/how-to**
