# Omega-3: Jesus vs Devil — Script v2 (Engaging Format)

## Principles applied
- **Hook in first 3 seconds** — curiosity gap, contrarian, or number
- **Devil is genuinely persuasive** — not a strawman, makes real points
- **Conflict & tension** — Jesus pushes back, Devil doubles down
- **Humor** — absurd comparisons, witty comebacks
- **Concrete stories** — not abstract facts
- **Surprise moments** — unexpected admissions, role reversals
- **Fast exchanges** → slow for truth reveal
- **Each scene self-contained** — viral-clip worthy

## Q1: "Is Omega-3 even important?"

### SCENE 1.1 — HOOK (Jesus)
**JESUS**: "Nine out of ten people watching this are deficient in something that could save their heart. Want to guess what?"

### SCENE 1.2 — Devil deflects with real point
**DEVIL**: "Oh, I love this game. It's Omega-3, right? Everyone says Omega-3. But here's what nobody mentions — Omega-6 does the same job, and it's already in everything you eat. Why supplement something you're already getting?"

### SCENE 1.3 — Jesus pushes back
**JESUS**: "Because 'already getting it' doesn't mean 'enough of it'."

### SCENE 1.4 — Devil doubles down (genuinely persuasive)
**DEVIL**: "Exactly! You're already getting too much. Dr. Layne Norton did a meta-analysis — polyunsaturated fats lower cancer mortality by 6%. Omega-6 IS a polyunsaturated fat. The science literally says it's good for you. Why are we fixing what isn't broken?"

### SCENE 1.5 — Jesus catches the trick
**JESUS**: "Wait. That study compared polyunsaturated fats to saturated fats. Not Omega-6 to Omega-3."

### SCENE 1.6 — Devil smile (caught but unfazed)
**DEVIL**: *(smiles)* "Tomato, tomato. They're both PUFAs. Your body doesn't read the label."

### SCENE 1.7 — TRUTH CUTAWAY (Devil to camera)
**DEVIL**: "Okay, I conflated two things. Classic move. Here's what's actually happening — Omega-6 and Omega-3 are both essential. But they're not the same. Omega-6 produces inflammatory prostaglandins. Omega-3 produces anti-inflammatory ones. You need both. But the ratio? Western diet is 20 to 1. Should be 4 to 1."

### SCENE 1.8 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "At 20 to 1, your body is in constant low-grade inflammation. That's not my opinion — that's basic biochemistry. Every cell membrane is packed with Omega-6, and the inflammatory cascade never stops."

### SCENE 1.9 — TRUTH: what to do
**DEVIL**: "So here's what actually works: cut seed oils — soybean, corn, sunflower. Eat sardines twice a week. Take one gram of EPA plus DHA daily. Get your Omega-3 index tested. Aim for 8 to 12 percent. That's it. That's the whole thing."

## Q2: "Does Omega-3 actually help your heart?"

### SCENE 2.1 — HOOK (Jesus)
**JESUS**: "A Harvard study followed 25,000 people for five years. One capsule a day. 28% fewer heart attacks. Is that real, or is that statistics?"

### SCENE 2.2 — Devil downplays (persuasive)
**DEVIL**: "It's statistics. 28% relative risk sounds impressive. But the absolute risk? Tiny. You know how many people in that study actually had a heart attack? Very few. The difference between 'very few' and 'slightly fewer very few' — that's your miracle."

### SCENE 2.3 — Jesus challenges
**JESUS**: "So you're saying Harvard wasted five years on nothing?"

### SCENE 2.4 — Devil cites counter-evidence
**DEVIL**: "Not nothing — just overhyped. Physionic reviewed 47 studies. Their conclusion? 'Small to moderate effect.' Even Mayo Clinic was lukewarm. When the mayo clinic says 'meh' about a supplement, maybe listen."

### SCENE 2.5 — Jesus has ammo
**JESUS**: "But Bill Harris — the guy who invented the Omega-3 index — showed people with the highest index had 35% lower heart disease risk. That's not 'small to moderate.' That's a sledgehammer."

### SCENE 2.6 — Devil deflects
**DEVIL**: *(waves hand)* "Bill Harris sells the test. Conflict of interest. Next."

### SCENE 2.7 — TRUTH CUTAWAY
**DEVIL**: "Okay, the Harris study is legit. Peer-reviewed, used worldwide. The Omega-3 index predicts cardiovascular death better than cholesterol. 35% lower risk, 15 to 18% lower all-cause mortality. I dismissed it because Harris sells the test — but the test works whether he sells it or not."

### SCENE 2.8 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "And that Physionic review I cited? It included low-quality studies with low doses and low bioavailability. Garbage in, garbage out. The VITAL trial used 1 gram daily across 25,871 people for 5 years — zero safety signals. Not 'some.' Zero. 28% fewer heart attacks. That's not statistics. That's science."

### SCENE 2.9 — TRUTH: what to do
**DEVIL**: "One gram of EPA plus DHA daily. That's the VITAL dose. If you have high triglycerides, your doctor may prescribe 3 to 4 grams. Get your Omega-3 index tested — aim for 8 to 12 percent. The US average is 5.4. That's not 'fine.' That's deficient."

## Q3: "What about the brain? Dementia? Alzheimer's?"

### SCENE 3.1 — HOOK (Jesus)
**JESUS**: "Your brain is 60% fat. A third of that fat is Omega-3. What happens when you don't have enough?"

### SCENE 3.2 — Devil minimizes
**DEVIL**: "Nothing dramatic. You forget where you put your keys. Same as everyone else. The brain is resilient — it converts ALA from flax seeds into DHA. Slow, but it works. You don't need fish."

### SCENE 3.3 — Jesus knows the number
**JESUS**: "The conversion rate is under 5%."

### SCENE 3.4 — Devil shrugs it off
**DEVIL**: "5% of a lot is still enough. Your body is smart. It regulates itself. Trust the process."

### SCENE 3.5 — Jesus pushes harder
**JESUS**: "The VITAL trial showed a 22% reduction in dementia symptoms. DHA is 97% of the Omega-3 in your brain. You're saying that's all... flax seeds?"

### SCENE 3.6 — Devil tries a different angle
**DEVIL**: *(leans forward)* "You know what else reduces dementia symptoms? Exercise. Sleep. Not being stressed. But those are free. You can't sell 'go for a walk' in a capsule."

### SCENE 3.7 — TRUTH CUTAWAY
**DEVIL**: "He's right that exercise and sleep matter. But DHA isn't just 'in' the brain — it IS the brain. It prevents brain shrinkage. It maintains every neuron's structural integrity. Dr. Michael Nelson at Karolinska showed Omega-3 regulates glial cells — the brain's immune cells — which clear beta-amyloid plaques. The literal hallmark of Alzheimer's."

### SCENE 3.8 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "And that 5% conversion I mentioned? For vegans, it's even worse. You'd need to eat a mountain of flax seeds to get the DHA in one can of sardines. 'Trust the process'? The process converts 5%. That's not a process — that's a leak."

### SCENE 3.9 — TRUTH: what to do
**DEVIL**: "For brain health, prioritize DHA. One gram of EPA plus DHA daily — the VITAL dose. If you're vegan, use algae-based DHA. Not flax oil — the conversion is too low. Dr. Sara Gottfried recommends an Omega-3 test for anyone with mood, focus, or memory issues, especially ages 35 to 50."

## Q4: "Can you take too much? What are the risks?"

### SCENE 4.1 — HOOK (Jesus)
**JESUS**: "Can something this good actually hurt you? What's the catch?"

### SCENE 4.2 — Devil lights up
**DEVIL**: "Oh, NOW you're asking the right question. Yes! Doses over 3 grams — atrial fibrillation. Especially if you already have heart disease. Siim Land himself warned about this. Omega-3 is a blood thinner. High doses, bleeding risk. This isn't a harmless supplement — it's pharmacologically active."

### SCENE 4.3 — Jesus concerned
**JESUS**: "That sounds serious. How much is too much?"

### SCENE 4.4 — Devil amplifies fear
**DEVIL**: "That's the problem — nobody agrees. Some say 1 gram, some say 3, some say 4. If experts can't agree, why risk it? I've seen people on high-dose Omega-3 who bruise from nothing."

### SCENE 4.5 — Jesus sees through it
**JESUS**: "But the VITAL trial — 25,000 people, 1 gram daily, 5 years — zero safety signals. You just told me that."

### SCENE 4.6 — Devil pivots
**DEVIL**: *(smiles)* "That was a different question. Now we're talking about risks. Different context. Don't mix contexts."

### SCENE 4.7 — TRUTH CUTAWAY
**DEVIL**: "Credit where due — this one is partially true. Doses over 3 grams CAN increase atrial fibrillation risk, especially with existing heart disease. That's real. But here's what I left out: the VITAL trial used 1 gram daily across 25,871 people for 5 years. Zero safety signals. The fibrillation risk is specific to high doses in heart patients."

### SCENE 4.8 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "For healthy people, 1 to 2 grams daily is extremely safe. The 3 to 4 gram dose is prescribed by doctors for high triglycerides — that's a medical intervention, not self-supplementation. I took a real but specific risk and amplified it to make ALL Omega-3 sound dangerous. Classic: grain of truth, mountain of fear."

### SCENE 4.9 — TRUTH: what to do
**DEVIL**: "1 to 1.5 grams of EPA plus DHA daily — safe for almost everyone. If you have heart disease, talk to your doctor before exceeding 1 gram. If you're on blood thinners, consult your doctor. Don't skip Omega-3 because of a risk that applies to 3 grams plus in heart patients."

## Q5: "Fish oil vs algae — does source matter?"

### SCENE 5.1 — HOOK (Jesus)
**JESUS**: "Fish oil, algae oil, krill oil — does it matter, or is it all the same molecule?"

### SCENE 5.2 — Devil says all the same
**DEVIL**: "It's all EPA and DHA. Your body doesn't care if it came from a fish, a plant, or a lab. Just buy whatever's cheapest. Actually — skip supplements entirely. Eat fish. But not too much fish, because mercury. So... you're stuck."

### SCENE 5.3 — Jesus catches the trap
**JESUS**: "That's a circular argument. 'Fish has mercury, supplements are scams, so do nothing.'"

### SCENE 5.4 — Devil leans back
**DEVIL**: *(smiles)* "Life is circular, my friend. Pick your poison."

### SCENE 5.5 — TRUTH CUTAWAY
**DEVIL**: "Source matters a lot. Fish oil contains biogenic amines — histamine triggers. Siim Land explained this: in histamine-sensitive people, fish oil causes palpitations and headaches. Algae-based Omega-3 skips that entirely. Same EPA and DHA, no amines."

### SCENE 5.6 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "Then there's the form. Triglyceride form has 70% better bioavailability than ethyl ester. JJ Virgin confirmed TG form is preferred. Enteric-coated capsules prevent fish burps. Distilled oil removes PCBs and dioxins. And the mercury argument? Sardines and mackerel are LOW mercury. FDA confirms this."

### SCENE 5.7 — TRUTH: what to do
**DEVIL**: "If you tolerate fish oil, choose triglyceride-form, enteric-coated, molecularly distilled. If you're histamine-sensitive, switch to algae-based. If you're vegan, algae oil is your only direct EPA/DHA source — flax converts at under 5%. Eat sardines twice a week — they're cheap, low-mercury, and packed with Omega-3."

**DEVIL**: *(wide smile)* "'Balanced diet.' My favorite words in the English language."

### SCENE 6.5 — Jesus presses
**JESUS**: "You really don't want people taking Omega-3, do you?"

### SCENE 6.6 — Devil's honest moment
**DEVIL**: *(long pause, then smile)* "I just want people to be... free. Free from overthinking. Free from supplements. Free from worry."

### SCENE 6.7 — TRUTH CUTAWAY (final, drops the smile)
**DEVIL**: "Here's the honest answer. General health: 1 gram of EPA plus DHA daily. That's the VITAL trial dose — 25,871 people, 28% fewer heart attacks, 22% fewer dementia symptoms. Optimal Omega-3 index: 1.5 grams daily plus fish twice a week. High triglycerides: 3 to 4 grams — but that's a medical dose, not self-medication."

### SCENE 6.8 — TRUTH continues
**DEVIL**: "Mood and brain: 1 to 2 grams daily. EPA for mood, DHA for cognition. Vegans: algae-based, not flax. Safety ceiling: don't exceed 3 grams without a doctor, especially with heart disease."

### SCENE 6.9 — TRUTH: the real lie
**DEVIL**: "'Zero' is never the right answer. 'Balanced diet' is what I say when I want people to do nothing. The most effective lie isn't the dramatic one — it's the quiet one. 'You don't need it.' That's the one that kills slowly."
